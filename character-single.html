Biography

Born in Shiganshina District, Eren grew up believing that humanity had been wiped out beyond the Walls. That illusion shattered the day the Colossal Titan broke through Wall Maria — the day his mother was devoured in front of him.

Driven by an all-consuming hatred, Eren enlisted in the 104th Training Corps and later joined the Survey Corps. What set him apart wasn't just his rage — it was his ability to transform into a Titan. A power that made him both a weapon and a target.

"If you win, you live. If you lose, you die. If you don't fight, you can't win."

As the series unfolds, Eren's worldview shifts from hopeful liberator to a figure willing to bear any burden. His decisions — some heroic, many terrible — reshape the fate of every character in the story.
