About Fan Hub Plus

One home for every fandom you love — Anime, Gaming, Movies, TV Shows, K-Pop, Comics, Manga, and Cosplay, all under one roof.

Our Story

Fan Hub Plus was built out of a simple frustration: fandom content is scattered everywhere — forums, social pages, niche wikis — and none of them talk to each other. If you love anime AND K-Pop AND cosplay, you end up juggling five different tabs just to keep up. We wanted one place that respects all your fandoms equally, without ads getting in the way of the content you actually came for.

Our Mission

To be the single, visually rich information hub where fans discover, explore, and celebrate the universes they love — through curated articles, character profiles, event listings, media galleries, and a community that gets it.

What You'll Find Here

  • Curated, categorized content across 8 major fandom domains
  • Powerful search, sorting, and filtering across every content type
  • Image galleries, trailers, soundtracks, and behind-the-scenes media
  • Character profiles and storytelling-style featured articles
  • A calendar of conventions, premieres, and meetups near you
  • A space to submit your own fan content and get featured

Built By Fans, For Fans

Every corner of Fan Hub Plus is designed by people who genuinely spend their weekends marathoning shows, chasing pre-orders, and standing in convention lines. We built the site we always wished existed.
